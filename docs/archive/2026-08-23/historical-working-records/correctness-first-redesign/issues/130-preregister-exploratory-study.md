# CF-130 — Pre-register the exploratory product and granularity study

- **Status:** `ready-for-agent`
- **Blocked by:** CF-120, CF-121, CF-122

## Outcome

Freeze an exploratory study before execution using a new medium TypeScript
application with meaningful domain, API, persistence, UI, seams, integration,
and independent product/browser/clean-clone oracles. Compare the canonical
adaptive granularity policy offline with predeclared coarse and fixed-boundary
baselines on frozen goals.

## Acceptance

- Hypotheses, scope, units, target repository/base, goals, model/profiles,
  prompts, budgets, policies, order/randomization, stop rules, invalidation rule,
  metrics, missing-data semantics, and analysis are frozen and hashed.
- Product correctness and topology/granularity oracles are independent of the
  implementing agents and distinguish smoke from evidence.
- No claim of universal optimality, statistical superiority, agent-singleton
  comparison, or node-count quality is pre-authorized.
- Study execution is forbidden until independent preregistration review passes.
