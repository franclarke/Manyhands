# CF-132 — Reconcile and report the exploratory study

- **Status:** `ready-for-agent`
- **Blocked by:** CF-131

## Outcome

Create the immutable final study report and claim-evidence ledger without
retrofitting adverse cells or overstating exploratory results.

## Acceptance

- Every planned cell is reconciled as satisfied, failed, inconclusive, not_run,
  not_applicable, or invalidated with exact evidence.
- Results address feasibility/product correctness and observed granularity
  trade-offs separately.
- Threats to internal, construct, and external validity; model variance; sample
  size; cost; target dependence; and operator influence are explicit.
- Conclusions remain bounded to the observed runs and preserve negative results.
